💵 Monthly cash-flow reality

Hong Kong has no payroll tax withholding — your employer pays you gross minus MPF, and the tax bill arrives later as a lump sum. A 13th month and bonus also arrive as lumps, not monthly. This is what a normal month actually looks like.

🧨 Your first tax bill

The classic newcomer trap: the first demand note charges your first (part) year plus provisional tax for the whole next year — at once.
Timing is typical, not guaranteed: returns are issued in May, demand notes usually follow in autumn, with ~75% due around January and ~25% around April. Provisional tax for a part first year is normally annualised to 12 months. When you leave Hong Kong for good, all outstanding tax must be cleared before departure (your employer withholds final pay until it is).
